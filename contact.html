MADEBYAPO

MADEBYAPO is a Switzerland-based designer and director working across visual identity, art direction and music. He is currently studying Interactive Media Design at Eikon, specializing in visual communication, and moves between graphic design, image and motion to build visual worlds that grow out of the music they serve.

He manages Swiss artist SARABDS and co-founded People of Gaza.
He has deep roots in French-speaking Switzerland’s music and creative scene, and his work is driven by rhythm, atmosphere and a sharp eye for detail. He is now turning toward music videos, where design, sound and storytelling come together.
